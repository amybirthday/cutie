Talking to you, getting happy over your little things, and dreaming about beautiful moments together means more to me than words can explain.

I know we are not perfect. There may be times when we disagree, get upset with each other, or misunderstand one another. We are both human, and making mistakes is a part of life.

I never want our little arguments or misunderstandings to become bigger than the beautiful relationship we share. I want us to celebrate each other's happiness, support each other through difficult times, and make our bond stronger with every passing day. 🥺❤️

I don't expect you to be perfect, and I don't expect our relationship to be perfect either. All I wish for is that we remain sincere with each other, respect each other's feelings, and protect our relationship with love, trust, patience, and understanding.

On your birthday, my biggest prayer is that Allah fills your life with endless happiness, grants you everything your heart wishes for, keeps that beautiful smile on your face forever, and protects you from every pain and sadness.

And lastly, I just want to say something from my heart:

I don't know what the future holds for us, but I truly hope we face every chapter of life by understanding each other, supporting each other, and choosing each other every single day. I want us to look back one day and smile at all the beautiful memories we created together. ❤️

I don't just want beautiful moments with you; I want us to learn, grow, understand, and become better together.

Happy Birthday, My Love! 🎂❤️

Always stay happy, keep smiling, and never forget how special you are.

You are special, your happiness matters, and I genuinely wish to see you happy, today and always. 🥹❤️
